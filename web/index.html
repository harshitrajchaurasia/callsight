Every client call, turned into checked facts and next steps.

Account managers at a lead agency talk to clients all day. After each call someone has to write notes, spot who is about to leave or ready to buy more, and log what was promised. CallSight does it in seconds, and shows the exact words behind every point, so nobody has to take the AI's word for it.

How it works

    Sign in

    Each call costs a little in AI usage, so this copy is invite-only. Request a login at harshit.saakura.dev/projects, or run it yourself from GitHub.

    Calls

    None yet. Process a call to see it here.

    Process a call

    Paste a transcript or upload a recording. Text takes about 10 seconds; audio about 20 seconds per minute.

    Stereo works best: one speaker per channel tells it who said what.

    No recording? Download a test call: short (1 min), quarterly review (4 min), consent complaint (3 min), expansion (3 min).

    Change

    Shared demo: anything you process is visible to every visitor. Use a test call or a made-up one, never real customer data. Up to 10 calls and 30 questions an hour.

    Ask across all calls

    Answers come only from what was said on the calls, with a link to each source.

    Settings

    Saved in this browser and used for every call and question you send from it.

    Theme

    Also on the button in the top bar.

    AI model

    Which model reads the masked call and answers questions.

    Masking

    What PII Shield hides before the AI sees a call.

    Phone numbers, emails, card, account and ID numbers are always masked. Company names are never masked.

    Churn alert

    Alert the account manager when the churn risk is at or above this.

    Applies to calls processed from now on. Default .
