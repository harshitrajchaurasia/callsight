Free and open source · demo with made-up clients

Every client call, turned into checked facts and next steps.

Account managers at a lead agency talk to clients all day. After each call someone has to write notes, spot who is about to leave or ready to buy more, and log what was promised. CallSight does it in seconds, shows the exact words behind every point, and lets a person check it before anything reaches the CRM.

How it works

    Sign in

    Each call costs a little in AI usage, so this copy is invite-only. Request a login at harshit.saakura.dev/projects, or run it yourself from GitHub.

    Today

    All processed calls

    Every call CallSight has analysed. Open one to see its summary, alerts and follow-ups, and to approve it.

    Risk of leaving is the AI's estimate of how likely the client is to stop buying, from what was said. Waiting for review means nobody has checked the AI's draft yet, so nothing has gone to Monday or the CRM.

    Process a call

    Paste a transcript, upload a recording, or record one here. Results take about 10 seconds; an uploaded recording about 20 seconds more per minute.

    Stereo works best: one speaker per channel tells it who said what.

    No recording? Download a test call: short (1 min), quarterly review (4 min), consent complaint (3 min), expansion (3 min).

    Uses this device's microphone, up to 10 minutes. One microphone hears everyone as one speaker, so the AI works out who is who; for a phone call, put it on speaker. Audio isn't kept: each few seconds is transcribed and deleted.

    Change

    Demo: calls you process are visible to you and the admin; the test calls are shared. Use a test call or a made-up one, never real customer data. Up to 10 calls and 30 questions an hour.

    Voice to action

    Say what needs doing. CallSight works out the tasks and notes, you check them, and only what you confirm is saved: tasks go to Monday, notes go on the client's record in the CRM (GHL).

    People are matched to the team list () and clients to the client list in code, never guessed. A person is named in the Monday item's title, not set in a people column, so nobody is notified.
