Callsight

Turns a recorded client call into a checked summary, alerts and follow-up tasks, and lets you search everything your clients have said.

Demo for a made-up lead agency, Acme Leads, whose account managers call the insurance agents who buy its leads. After each call someone has to write notes, spot a client who is about to leave or ready to buy more, and log what was promised. Callsight does that in seconds.
